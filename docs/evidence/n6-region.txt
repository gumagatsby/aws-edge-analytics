The only resources outside eu-west-2 are the CloudFront log delivery source, destination and delivery
(terraform/logging.tf), which CloudWatch Logs requires in us-east-1. They hold configuration, not data:
logs are delivered straight to the raw bucket in eu-west-2. Glue, Athena, Lake Formation, Step Functions,
the Glue Data Catalog and the KMS key are all in the eu-west-2 provider.
